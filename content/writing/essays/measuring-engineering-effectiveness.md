## Start with the decision

Which decision should a metric help the team make? Collect examples where a measurement changed a useful conversation, and examples where it distracted from one.

## Questions to explore

- What can the team change in response to the measure?
- What context is missing from the number?
- How could optimizing the measure make the work worse?
- Which qualitative observations belong alongside it?

## Evidence to gather

Add references, a concrete team scenario, and counterexamples before developing the argument.
