This notebook brings together four connected interests: software engineering, AI engineering, leadership, and building products.

Not every idea needs to become an essay. Sometimes a short note is enough to capture something useful. Sometimes an experiment raises more questions than it answers. Those belong here too.

## The shape of the notebook

- **Notes** capture small discoveries and working ideas.
- **Essays** give an opinion room to develop.
- **Guides** collect explanations worth returning to.
- **Labs** document experiments and things taking shape.

The goal is simple: turn more of the learning that happens along the way into something durable.

This is a starting point. The notebook will grow with the work.
