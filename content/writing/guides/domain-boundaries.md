A guide outline to develop through examples and further reading.

## Start with the language

Choose a small domain and record how people describe the work. Look for terms that carry different meanings in different conversations.

## Follow a change

Trace one change across the system and the people involved. Document where information passes between teams and where decisions are made.

## Make the tradeoffs explicit

Use a concrete example to compare possible boundaries. Record the assumptions, costs, and questions that remain open.
